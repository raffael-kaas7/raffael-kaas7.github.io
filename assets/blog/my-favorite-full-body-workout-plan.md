Normally always full body. Serveral reasons. I like the feeling more having trained all body parts, than just a single body part like chest, back or legs. Another reason is that I like to keep frequency of my training sessions per body part high. 2-3 weeks each muscle. Then only full body makes sense because I dont want to go 6 days to the gym. I also like go out for a run or do some yoga session for my mobility and streching. 

Here is my full body workout plan that I love. Sometimes I choose othere exercises, but those mentioned below, I'd stick to foreever. They are fun, most of them are compounding exercises, and then support functional strength that supports you during your day-to-day activities. 

## Day A

#### Back Squads 
#### Hip Thrust

#### Pull Up (weighted)
#### Incline bench press

#### Cable Row
#### Butterfly

Only if some power left
#### Cable Pushdown
#### Hammer Curls


## Day B

#### Deadlifts
#### Leg Press

#### Pull up (weighted)
#### Chin up (weighted)

#### Bench Press
#### Dips (weighted)

Only if some power left
#### Cable Pushdown 
#### Barbell Curls

For core and lower back, I do plank, leg raises, superman from time to time. For me that is enough.
