Everyone has experienced being so absorbed in something that you forget everything around you. You look at the clock and cannot believe how much time has passed. Everyone knows the feeling, but might never have thought much about what it actually is or how we can make it happen by choice. This is what Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi explores in his famous book.

**Flow:** The task challenges us, but we have a good chance of completing it. We can concentrate, have a clear goal, and get immediate feedback on how we are doing. We feel in control of our actions. Even when the task takes effort, being involved in it can feel effortless. Everyday worries and frustrations temporarily disappear from our awareness. We also stop thinking so much about ourselves and how we appear to others. Afterwards, we can come away with a stronger sense of ourselves and our abilities (complexity of the self). Our sense of time changes too: hours can feel like minutes, or a brief moment can seem to stretch out.

That's it. So all we need is a real challenge that does neither overchallenging nor underchallenging our capabilities, but offer space for growth. We need a clear goal and something that gives immediate feedback in that specific activity. 

*hier is die überleitung noch holprig* 

Csikszentmihalyi describes this as 'optimal experience'. People seek happiness above everthing else. Already the ancient greeks like Aristotle thought about that. Nevertheless, unitol today we do not really know what it means and what supports seeking happines. Knowing that there is no unique recipe for happniess to copy Csikszentmihalyi draws on his whole research about optimal experience (Flow) as one ... *help me here* to achieve happiniess. Instead of belongings, status and pleasure, the optimal experience and its conditions lead to personal growth and more complex individuals. 

## My flow experiences

The book explores a few different sources of flow: The body, thought, work, solitude, and other people. After reading it I want to take the time and reflect on my personal flow activities and how to increase optimal experience.

I think it is important not to focus on one specific area and neglect the others instead. Having a good mix of flow activities across all areas looks like good to enjoy live in general. 

### Body

This is probably the area where I am since every natrually enjoying optimal experience in various flow experiences, because I am doing a lot of sports since early childhood. No matter which sport, it is always something about *höher, schneller, weiter*. Even in more sufisticated sports like footbool. The goal is a bit different, but in the end you want to get better with your team and in training as well as matched you have some kind of feeback. In the past I was more into playing footbol 3 weeks per week in a team competing against others all over the year. 

In the meantime I have replaced playing footbool with running and strength training. In running it is all about beating my best time in the next marathon and in strength training it is not about gaining musscles (this is just a nice byproduct), but about getting better in Pull ups, bench press and back squats. 

Something that is not that mature but I want to focus more on is calisthenics. Not only being strong and enduraned, but also having his body in control. It is just impressive what our nervous system can do, if we just train it. I know that I find joy in exercising skills like pushing into handstand and some other cool calesthenics skills, it is just not yet a deep habit of me.

### Work

I am a Software Engineer and obviusly coding was my passion and source of optimal experience. Headfones on, some slow techno and time for deep work. Choosing the right data structure, breaking down bigger tasks into smaller problems and implementing complex algorithms with some basic loops. Being happy after sucessfull compilation, getting frustated during debugging and finally having a running software like intended. Coding is by nature what Mihaly descibes as Fow and optimal experiences. 

Somehow this is gone. Coding is solved by AI. Somehow this is really sad. For now I am not up to coding at home without AI just for the sake of doing it for enjoyment, as kind of a art. I am not sure if this would be the same as before. I just know that it made work a really enjoyable for me.

It is nothing that you can reproduce on a daily level which is really enjoyable.

## Summary

I want to come back to the difference between pleasure and enjoyment. Eating when hungry gives pleasure by satisfying a need. An optimal experience asks something of us and can leave us with new abilities or understanding. This is part of what Csikszentmihalyi means by a more complex self: becoming more capable as an individual and more connected to the world around us.

For me, the question to explore is how much attention I give to what I own and how much to what I actually experience. Belongings can support activities I enjoy, but having them alone does not create that enjoyment. Flow offers a way to participate more fully in everyday life. I want to see where that could contribute to my own happiness.
