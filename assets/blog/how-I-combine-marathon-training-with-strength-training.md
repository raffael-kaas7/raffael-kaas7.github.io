I have seasons with different focus. 

marathon preparation. Usually 3-4 months in advance, depending on how fit I stayed "off season".
Off season means that I do not specifically train for a running event. During that time, I run 0-2 times a week, somewhere between 5-12 km each time. No special training like intervalls specificall for vo2max, but also no zone 2 training. Just with my comfortable running pace the full distance. Thi is mostly aerobic with a small anaerobic part. 

strength training, usually the winter months were I go heavily into the gym 
In summer I also like doing full body workouts outside at the calisthenics park. 

When I am training for a running event, I do not fully neglect strength training. I dont want to get weaker in my favorite exercise and dont want to loose a lot of body mass, although it is kind of unavoidable during marathon training. Even while eating as much as you can. 


Currently while writing I have a marathon in front of me in more or least 4 months. This time I went out of off season very fit, because I was running a half marathon together with friends a couple of weeks ago. 

Because of that I am currently still having my focus on strength training. I especially focus on legs, because I had the feeling that they were a bit of a weak point my last marathon. I do heavy back squats and some leg curls and leg extension for isolated trainings. I do this twice a week to be best prepared for switching focus on running. Currently I only run once per week, a long run with 20+ kilometers, but really low heart rate, zone 2 training to not cover my full rovery capacity. 

Around Christmas I want to switch focus and go to 2-3 runs, adding a quality run (e.g. intervall training) and if needed an easy recovery run to increase weekly volume.

Since I want to beat my personal best time of 3h.51 minutes I need to check for at least one half marathon in which pace I can comfortable run. I somehow neglected pace training the last months and my v02max decreased a bit. Nevertheless, in my last marathon the limiting facotr was not my cardiovascular system but the *aktiver und passiver bewegungsapperat*. Sure my heart rate went up, but my legs were the problem to keep pace. Thats why I focus more on leg training in advance, instead of doing more and more runs.

I don't aim to set a best time of 3h30min or something like that. I just want to beat my best time by minutes. I want to keep room for more in the future and somehow strength training is more important for me this time.

I am running the marathon in rome and I am really hyped about the event at all and the city sightseeing before. I've never been there before, so it is combining leisure with non-leisure :)
