Books

Books that shaped my thinking, ideas I want to remember, and a collection of notes I keep returning to.

For a couple of years, reading around 20 minutes before sleeping has become a habit. It calms me down and helps me get to sleep. I am not sure if this is best for remembering what I've read, but I enjoy it.

So far, I've mainly read nonfiction, much of it in the areas of personal development and finance. I imagine that will change in the future because I feel like I am currently saturated by these topics.
