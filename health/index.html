Health

All about workout, nutrition, recovery, and how I try staying healthy alongside normal work and life.

I am not (yet) a die-hard member of the longevity community, but I am definitely inspired by Peter Attia and his book 'Outlive'. I focus on muscle mass, VO2 max, sleep, and mental health.

I used to actively play soccer, but due to many injuries I decided to quit. Now I keep myself fit with running and gym training / calisthenics.

I am currently working on my own Gym Logging Tool with minimized screen time in the gym. It's a Progressive Web App, implemented through spec-driven development.
