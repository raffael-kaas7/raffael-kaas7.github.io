I want to use this as a light trip log before the memories disappear somewhere between camera roll, GPX files, and a few random notes.

In August 2026 I rode from Lindau at Lake Constance towards Lago Maggiore, carrying a tent and crossing the Alps by bike. This draft is only the structure for now. I still want to add pictures, small notes from each day, and a few thoughts about what this trip felt like.

The image above is an illustrated overview of the route. The numbers below come from the two GPX tracks from Wednesday and Thursday. Friday and Saturday were not tracked, so those distances are estimates for now.

## Route summary

- Trip dates: Wednesday, 12.08.2026 to Saturday, 15.08.2026.
- Main route: Lindau - Andeer - Bellinzona - Locarno / Lago Maggiore.
- Tracked cycling: 230.5 km.
- Estimated total cycling: roughly 260-280 km, depending on the exact Friday route and campsite detour.
- Tracked elevation gain: around 2,150 m.
- Tracked elevation loss: around 2,340 m.
- Highest recorded point: 2,062 m on the San Bernardino Pass.
- GPX moving-time estimate: around 13 h 30 min.
- Setup: bike, tent, and camping gear.
