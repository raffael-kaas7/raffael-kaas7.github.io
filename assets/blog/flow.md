*Einleitung passt auch nicht so ganz. irgdnwie unnatürlich, nicht mein stil*. I am pretty sure, everyone had ever experienced being so absorbed in something that you forget everything around you. You look at the clock and cannot believe how much time has passed. Everyone knows the feeling, but might never have thought much about what it actually is or how we can make it happen by choice. This is what Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi explores in his famous book Flow. Here is my definition after reading:

**Flow:** The activity challenges us, but we have a good chance of completing it. We can concentrate, have a clear goal, and get immediate feedback on how we are doing. We feel in control of our actions. Even when the task takes effort, being involved in it can feel effortless. Everyday worries and frustrations temporarily disappear from our awareness. We also stop thinking so much about ourselves and how we appear to others. Afterwards, we can come away with a stronger sense of ourselves and our abilities (complexity of the self). The sense of time often changes too: hours can feel like minutes, or a brief moment can seem to stretch out.

That's it. So all we need is a real challenge that does neither overchallenging nor underchallenging our capabilities, but offer space for growth. We need a clear goal and something that gives immediate feedback in that specific activity. 

*hier is die überleitung noch holprig* 

Csikszentmihalyi describes this as 'optimal experience' and one way to optimize overll happiness in live. People seek happiness above everthing else. Already the ancient greeks like Aristotle thought about that. Nevertheless, unitl today we do not really know what it means and what supports seeking happines. Knowing that there is no unique recipe for happniess to copy Csikszentmihalyi draws on his whole research about optimal experience (Flow) as one ... *help me here* to achieve happiniess. Instead of belongings, status and pure pleasure activities like eating and watching Tv on the couch, the optimal experience and its conditions lead to personal growth and more complex individuals. However they require often some discomfort in the first place although they are truely enjoyable in the end.

## My flow experiences

The book explores a few different sources of flow: The body, thought, work, solitude, and other people. After reading it I want to take the time and reflect on my personal flow activities and how to increase optimal experience.

I think it is important to not simply focus on one specific area and neglect the others instead. Having a good mix of flow activities across all areas looks like good way to enjoy live in general. 

<div class="section-break"></div>

### Body

This is probably the area where I am since every natrually enjoying optimal experience in various flow experiences, because I am doing a lot of sports since early childhood. 

No matter which sport, it is always something about *höher, schneller, weiter*. Even in more sufisticated sports like footbool. The goal is a bit different, but in the end you want to get better. Although it is with a team, you feel in control and in training as well as matched you have always immediate feeback on the performance. 

In the past I was more into playing footbol 3 weeks per week in a team competing against others all over the year. In the meantime I have replaced playing footbool with running and strength training. In running it is all about beating my best time in the next running event and in strength training, for me personally it is not about gaining musscles and looking in shape (this is just a nice byproduct), but about getting better in my favorite exercises: Pull ups, bench press and back squats. 

Something that is not that mature but I want to focus more on is calisthenics. Not only being strong and enduraned, but also having his body in control. It is just impressive what our nervous system can do, if we just train it. Consistently. I know that I find joy in exercising skills like pushing into handstand and some other sophistacted calesthenics skills, it is just not yet a deep habit of me.

Thinking about flow now, I should definitly pick up my guitar again more often. Somehow this got lost during the last few years. I think priorities just pushed it down. I probably should order my priorities again, now knowing more about flow.

### Work

*hier ist glaub der rote faden nicht optimal. Inhalte möchte ich mehr oder weniger behalten, aber die ordnung stimmt noch nicht ganz. Fazit: Aktuell, mein job mit weniger optimal experience, das was spaß gemacht hat hat keinen ökonimischen wert mehr. Fokus flow in anderen aktivitäten finden.* 

I am a Software Engineer and obviusly coding is my passion and source of optimal experience. Headfones on, some slow techno music and the deep work can start. Breaking down bigger tasks into smaller problems, choosing the right data structure and implementing complex algorithms. Being happy after sucessfull compilation, getting frustated during debugging and finally having a running software like intended. Coding is by nature what Mihaly descibes as Fow and optimal experiences. And so it was for me. 

Somehow this is gone. Currently coding looks like being solved by AI. At least my personal workflow has changed a lot in the agentic world. I am not much into deep work anymore. Instead, I need to force my self, not grabbing my cellphone when agents are running. 

Somehow this is really sad. For now I am not up to coding at home without AI just for the sake of doing it for enjoyment, as kind of a art. I am not sure if this would be the same as before, when it does not have a economic value to handwrite code. I just know that it made work a really enjoyable for me. I am not sure if this can be replaced. For sure you can brake it down to solving problems and problem solving will be there also with AI. For sure it is also rewarding to launch a great product or just reachign the next milestone, but it is on another abstraction level. It is nothing that you can reproduce on a daily level which is really enjoyable. Things now have broader, slower feedback like “the project moved forward.”

For now I dont really have an answer, deep work with AI is different than before and when I am writing this post, I am not having this optimal experience that often at work as in earlier times. The plan is to find flow in technical Investigatrions, good meetings, design and review taks, unblocking collegues to come back to a state where I like my job in a similar way then before coding agents.

I think understanding large code bases as well as complex functions and design patterns is still worth to do without AI support and could be as enjoybable as coding. Developers need to keep professient in understanding code in the furure (at least this is my personal opinion for now).
