Travel

Travel memories I want to keep instead of losing them in the cloud.

In my 20s, I had the privilege of traveling to many countries, also a few outside of Europe, which motivated me to get one of these 'scratch-off-where-you-have-been' world maps. I have a digital one.

Unfortunately, I didn't document everything as thoroughly as I did my trip through Australia. Setting up this space is meant to motivate me to do the same documentation work in the future, and hopefully it will also serve as inspiration for some of you.
